Kelly Grappe and David Adam Byrnes share more than Arkansas roots. Music, family farms, hometown festivals, and years of friendship came long before this campaign moment. David has brought his songs to the Grappe family farm, to Rose Bud, and to stages Kelly and Steve have supported throughout their marriage.

Chapter 1 — Arkansas made them

David built a life telling honest stories through country music — modern energy with traditional storytelling about love, perseverance, and staying grounded. Visit DavidAdamByrnes.com.

Kelly built a life in Rose Bud and across Arkansas — wife, mother, grandmother, farmer with Steve at Forevermost Farms, civic educator, and candidate for Secretary of State who believes people participate when they feel respected. Meet Kelly at KellyGrappe.com.

David performed at the Grappe / Forevermost Farms festival during two separate summers. Neighbors gathered, food was shared, and music became part of the rhythm of family life — not a one-time booking.
