Come home with us

Two Arkansas roads. One hometown night.

Sherwood helped shape Kelly Grappe. David Adam Byrnes grew the same Arkansas soil into songs about love, perseverance, and staying grounded. Their paths have crossed at farm festivals, hometown stages, and years of friendship — including two summers at the Grappe family farm and a Rose Bud festival appearance.

Kelly and Steve got engaged after a David Adam Byrnes show in Sherwood. They have watched him play and supported his career throughout their marriage. Now they are inviting Arkansas to sit down together again.
