Constitution Day celebration

What better day to celebrate the power of the people than Constitution Day? Grassroots & Guitar Strings is Thursday, September 17 — the anniversary of the signing of the U.S. Constitution.

We're joined by Sherwood's own David Adam Byrnes, recently named Arkansas Country Music Artist of the Year, for a hometown performance celebrating community, citizenship, and the voices that make Arkansas strong.

Come for country music, connect with friends old and new, and pick up yard signs, postcards, door hangers, campaign gear, and ways to help build a people-powered movement across Arkansas.
