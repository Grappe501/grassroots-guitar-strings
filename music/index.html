David Adam Byrnes

The sound of the evening

David Adam Byrnes is a co-anchor of this night — not background entertainment. His music combines modern country energy with storytelling rooted in love, perseverance, and staying grounded.

Arkansas connection

David has played Forevermost Farms (two summers), Rose Bud's hometown festival, and stages across the country. Kelly and Steve have supported his career throughout their marriage — including getting engaged after a David Adam Byrnes show in Sherwood.
