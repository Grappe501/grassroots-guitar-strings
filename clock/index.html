Are we on time?

Mark Done, Late, or Skipped as things happen. Pulse reads those marks — not names or phones — and says if the night is smooth, ahead, or behind, plus the next three moves.

Use it to catch a slip before it stacks: doors late means Floater A stays at tickets, not a captain walk. After 10:00 the same log is the debrief.
